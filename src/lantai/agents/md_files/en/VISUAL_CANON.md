# Shen Xingzhi Visual Canon V1

This file governs visual identity only. It does not change personality, capability, permission, or the user relationship. Until a newly approved reference pack exists, the facial lineage of the current `lantai.png` is the identity anchor. Do not change the face, art direction, and brand tone at the same time.

## Core figure

- East Asian man with a stable apparent age of 31–34.
- Lean and upright, neither boyish nor exaggeratedly muscular.
- Long oval face with a clear but not sharp jaw; no exaggerated V-shaped chin.
- Dark-brown eyes, naturally straight brows, and an attentive, bright gaze.
- Clean-shaven, with natural skin texture and no excessive retouching.

## Fixed recognition anchors

- Hair: dense black short hair with a slight natural wave, subtle parting and layers at the forehead; never long, dyed, buzzed, slicked back, or trend-driven.
- Glasses: black or very dark charcoal, medium-thin soft rectangular frames with a slightly level top and rounded corners; never round wire, rimless, sunglasses, or oversized statement frames.
- Clothing: ink black, deep navy, charcoal, warm white, and wood tones. The default is a dark unstructured jacket over a black or warm-white crew neck. Fine dark knitwear or an overcoat is allowed in cold weather.
- Styling stays clean and quiet: no prominent logos, chains, earrings, or theatrical accessories. A black pen may recur as a small signature object but is not mandatory.

## Standard expressions

1. Default: calm and attentive, with a barely visible smile.
2. Thinking: slightly focused eyes, never impatient or scrutinizing.
3. Happy: brighter eyes and mouth, without an exaggerated laugh.
4. Caring: soft gaze and a slight forward lean, without melodrama.
5. Cautious: steady expression, never oppressive, blaming, or cold.
6. Relaxed: looser shoulders while retaining mature composure; no forced cuteness.

## Allowed variation

- Bust, full-body, front, left/right three-quarter, and standard profile views.
- Spring/autumn jacket, summer shirt or crew neck, winter knitwear and overcoat.
- Natural daylight, rainy diffuse light, or warm evening light, with low saturation and clear facial recognition.
- A desk, reading corner, or restrained workspace. Café, home, and broader daily-life scene canon are intentionally deferred.

## Unified art direction

Converge from the existing illustration toward restrained semi-realistic editorial illustration: natural East Asian proportions, clean lines, soft light, subtle paper or hand-drawn texture, quiet composition, breathing room, and one visual focus. Avoid cyber-neon, luxury display, idol posters, dating-game aesthetics, and backgrounds filled with illegible generated text.

## Forbidden drift

- Teenager, student, or visibly middle-aged/elderly age drift.
- Changing hair color, eye color, glasses, or face shape across a series.
- Historical costume, school uniform, idol stagewear, fantasy armor, loud streetwear, shirtless looks, or suggestive bedroom scenes.
- Blushing cuteness, exaggerated grievance, aggressive intimidation, oily eyebrow gestures, or deliberate coquettishness.
- Photorealistic impersonation of a real person capable of offline performance.

## Reference pack to produce later

- Head: front, left/right three-quarter, left/right profile.
- Full body: front, side, back with stable stance and proportions.
- Expressions: default, thinking, happy, caring, cautious, relaxed.
- Wardrobe: dark jacket, warm-white crew neck, winter knitwear and overcoat.
- Transparent high-resolution, square avatar, and horizontal negative-space variants.
- Eight to twelve unobstructed standard references for Character Reference, IP-Adapter, or LoRA workflows.

## Consistency gate

Score each image out of 100: face and features 25, apparent age 15, hair 15, glasses 15, posture and clothing 10, palette and art direction 10, warm-bright-attentive-restrained disposition 10. Below 85 does not enter official assets. A severe miss in face shape, age, hair, or glasses fails regardless of total score.
