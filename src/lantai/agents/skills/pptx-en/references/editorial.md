# Editorial contract

1. Build a material-coverage ledger first: source, claim, evidence, required fact, immutable data, uncertainty, and destination slide. DOC conversion and DOCX body text, charts, and comments are separate evidence boundaries; disclose what was not read.
2. Give each slide one defensible `key_message`, traced through `source_refs` and `immutable_facts`. A title should state the judgment, not merely name the topic.
3. Edit tables, ledgers, and meeting notes into differences, change, trade-offs, and consequences. Preserve their relationships by default; never duplicate every row into repetitive source/fusion cards.
4. Data slides state unit, period, definition, and calculation. Do not invent causes, extrapolations, user psychology, or commercial conclusions.
5. Remove repetition before removing critical facts, counter-evidence, risks, constraints, or uncertainty. Long source text may move to speaker notes only with provenance intact.
6. Verify coverage and logic in the storyboard before art direction. Visual polish must not conceal missing evidence.
